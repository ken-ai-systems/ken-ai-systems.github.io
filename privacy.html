Privacy Policy

Ken — personal AI Chief of Staff. Last updated October 24, 2026.

Who this is for

Ken is a private, personal application. Its only user is its owner. It is not offered to the public, and it does not sign in anyone else.

What Ken accesses

With the owner's permission, Ken uses Google sign-in to read and update files in the owner's own Google account: Google Drive, Docs and Sheets. It uses that access only to do the owner's own work, such as reading the owner's documents and keeping the owner's records up to date.

Where the data goes

  • Data stays in the owner's own Google account and on the owner's own private server.
  • Ken does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • Ken does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not use it to train general AI models.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's server and are never published.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Ken's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Ken's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Yahoo Fantasy Sports data

Ken reads Yahoo Fantasy Sports data only for its owner's own team and league, with read-only access the owner grants through Yahoo sign-in. It reads league settings, standings, rosters, matchups, player stats and status, free agents and transactions, only when the owner asks a fantasy question. Ken does not post or change anything on Yahoo. It does not sell, rent or share Yahoo data with anyone, does not use it for advertising or to train general AI models, and keeps access credentials only on the owner's private server. The owner can revoke access at any time in their Yahoo account settings.
